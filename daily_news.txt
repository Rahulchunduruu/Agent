Jantar Mantar, Delhi (27 July 2026):  
"The 36-day NEET-paper-leak protest has ended."  
"Protesters dispersed after Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan resigned."  
"The site is now quiet."  
"Internet service has been restored."  
"Delhi Metro services are running normally again." 
"Indian government response steps:"  
"1. Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan resigned (26 July 2026)."  
"2. 47 NTA officials were terminated as part of a sweeping overhaul."  
"3. Fast-track courts were ordered for paper-leak cases and stricter punishments proposed."  
"4. Union Minister Kiren Rijiju briefed Parliament that the government acted immediately."  
"5. NTA governance was restructured with a new director-general and tighter audits."  
"6. Ministry of Education assured NEET 2026 results on schedule with enhanced security measures."  
"7. Internet service and Delhi Metro operations were restored after the protest ended."
